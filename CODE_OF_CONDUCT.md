# Community conduct

This project welcomes respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

- Discuss code and ideas constructively. Give actionable feedback and assume good intent.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, spam or publish another person's private information.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant and follow the security reporting policy.
- Respect requests to stop disruptive behavior.

The maintainer, @mes113, may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, close discussions,
or restrict participation. Responses should reflect the conduct and context, rather than
a contributor's background or technical experience. For private reports of abusive conduct,
use GitHub's Report abuse controls. Security vulnerabilities belong in the private channel
linked from [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
